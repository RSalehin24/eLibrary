## Rules

- A gate cannot be waived silently: a waiver is a dated entry in [waivers](waivers.md) with owner and expiry of at most 30 days.
- Evidence is reproducible: each item links to a CI run, a report or a file in the repository. Evidence that needs the gold corpus is a local report, never the corpus itself.
- If a gate fails twice, stop and re-plan the phase (scope reduction first, not gate relaxation).
- No gate has been passed yet; nothing in this file is evidence.
